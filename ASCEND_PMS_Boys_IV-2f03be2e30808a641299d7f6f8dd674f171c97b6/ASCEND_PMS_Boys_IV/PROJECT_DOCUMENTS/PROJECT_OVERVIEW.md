# ASCEND Project Overview

ASCEND is the gamified student challenge and recognition platform designed for **Peshawar Model School Boys IV**. Students activate accounts with their admission number and a school-issued activation code, add an optional school-appropriate profile picture, complete challenges, submit evidence, earn category points, climb leaderboards, read announcements and claim rewards.

Administration is entirely no-code:

- The **Super Admin** is the protected owner account with full access to staff-admin management, branding, production controls, backups and all school operations.
- **Staff Admins** can be created by the Super Admin and given selected operational permissions such as students/points, challenges, achievements, rewards, announcements and activity logs.
- Staff admins cannot access protected Super Admin controls even through direct URLs.

Brand: orange + white + black. Category accents: academic blue, sports green, character gold, activity teal. School crest remains visually separate from ASCEND app branding.

## v1.3 presentation layer

ASCEND includes a professional public landing page, About and Privacy pages for school presentation, protected no-code CSV reporting for student progress and challenge participation, and a Super Admin system/version status view. These additions do not change the core database model or onboarding workflow.

ASCEND at {{ settings.school_name }}

ASCEND is a school challenge and recognition platform designed to make positive competition visible, organized and easy to manage.

What students do

Students activate a school-created account, join eligible challenges, submit work where required, earn verified points and follow their progress through leaderboards, achievements and rewards.

What admins do

Trusted school administrators manage student access, challenges, points, rewards and announcements through no-code controls. Sensitive owner controls remain restricted to the Super Admin.

Competition categories

ASCEND supports Academic, Sports, Character and Activity categories while keeping one consistent school-wide ranking and interface.

Our design goal

The platform is built to encourage effort and participation without turning school life into a noisy game. Recognition stays clear, auditable and school-controlled.

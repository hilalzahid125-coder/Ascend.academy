STUDENT DATA & PRIVACY

Privacy in ASCEND

ASCEND is designed as a school-controlled platform. Student information should only be used for legitimate school participation, recognition and administration.

Data the platform may hold

Admission number, student name, class and section, account credentials in hashed form, challenge activity, point history, achievements, reward claims, notifications and an optional profile picture.

Who can access it

Students can access their own account and relevant school competition information. Authorized admins receive only the operational permissions assigned to them. Super Admin controls are separately protected.

Profile pictures

Profile pictures are optional. Students and school staff should follow the school’s photo/privacy policy before using real student images. The system validates and re-encodes uploaded images before storing them.

Passwords and security

Passwords are not stored in readable form. ASCEND uses hashed passwords, server-side sessions, role checks, CSRF protection, rate limiting and auditable administrator actions.

Data retention and backups

School administrators control student accounts and records. Super Admin can create downloadable backups for continuity and disaster recovery. Old accounts should be disabled or removed according to school policy.

Questions or corrections

Students should contact the school administrator if their account details are incorrect, they need account access reset, or they have a concern about information shown in ASCEND.

Important: ASCEND is a technical platform, not a replacement for the school’s official privacy policy or legal obligations.

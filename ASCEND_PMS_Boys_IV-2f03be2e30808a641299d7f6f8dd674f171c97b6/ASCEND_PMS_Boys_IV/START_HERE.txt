ASCEND — PMS Boys IV v1.2
=========================

This package is the production-hardened build with student profile pictures,
Super Admin / Staff Admin delegation, bulk roster onboarding and full backups.

Railway:
1. Keep the persistent volume mounted at /data.
2. Deploy this code from GitHub.
3. Keep PMS_SECURE_COOKIE=1 and PMS_DATA_DIR=/data.
4. Sign in with the existing main admin; it becomes the protected Super Admin.
5. Use "Admin team" to create trusted Staff Admins and choose their permissions.
6. Use "Launch & backups" for production preparation and full ZIP backups.
7. Bulk-import the real student roster when available.
8. Enable student activation only when the roster and activation codes are ready.

Do not run a demo seed script in production; demo seed files are intentionally not included.
